Tonkatsu Box is a free, open-source app to organize your personal media collections — games, movies, TV shows, anime, visual novels, comics and manga, all in one place.

Search millions of titles from online databases, track your progress, rate everything, and shape your library into visual boards and mood grids.

Features:

* Unified search across many sources: IGDB, TMDB, TVmaze, VNDB, AniList, MangaBaka, MangaDex, Kitsu, OpenLibrary, Fantlab, ComicVine and Google Books.
* Build collections and organize them your way — grids, sorting, filters and bulk editing.
* Track progress and episodes, rate items, and keep detailed notes.
* Visual boards and mood grids to arrange your collection the way you like.
* Import your library from Steam, IGDB lists, Trakt.tv, Kinorium, RetroAchievements, MyAnimeList or AniList.
* Local SQLite storage with backup and restore — your data stays on your device.
* Fully localized interface with runtime language switching (English, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French).

Privacy:

Tonkatsu Box has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your collection is stored locally on your device. The app talks to third-party media databases (such as IGDB, TMDB and others) only to search for titles and fetch cover art and metadata.

Some search sources are proprietary online services; you can optionally supply your own API keys in Settings.
