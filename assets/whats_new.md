# 0.46.0

**めんどくせ 0.46**

**Cards show who made them.** Directors and studios on movies, creators and networks on series, developers and publishers on games. Tap a name to find the rest of its work in your collection. For cards you added earlier, press "Refresh from source".

**Refresh many cards at once.** Select cards in a collection or in All Items and press "Refresh from source" in the action bar. They refresh one by one with a progress window you can stop at any point. Fantlab and Hardcover books keep their edition.

**Zoom the whole app.** Ctrl + wheel, Ctrl + plus and Ctrl + minus scale the interface from 50% to 200%, Ctrl + 0 brings it back. On a phone, use the "Interface scale" slider in Settings → Appearance.

- A collection remembers where its imports came from. The import button inside it offers each source again and reruns it into the same collection.
- Linux builds ship as an AppImage next to the tar.gz: make it executable and run it.
- Badges on a card grow with the card, so on a phone they are easier to hit.
- Filter chips, table filters and sort menus show hover and keyboard focus.
- Custom cards get an Authors field, also in the CSV and JSON import.
- Title search finds a movie by its director and a game by its developer.
- Two new themes: PS1 Retro and Eva Azure, by shingo110.
- Kitsu moved to kitsu.app; requests and links from a card follow it.
- Ctrl+F puts the cursor in the search field.
- Emptied description, alternative title, genres and link of a custom card are saved.
- Episode stills come back after a broken download.
- Cache cleanup removes covers of deleted custom cards.
- Collection import tells you how many custom cards it skipped.
- After "Refresh from source", collections and search show the new data, and a refresh that fails keeps the old cover.
