# 0.45.0

**Midnight Shinigami 0.45**

- Custom cards import can look your rows up in the catalogs: TMDB, IGDB, AniList, Kitsu, VNDB, book sources and more. A row with exactly one match by title, year and platform becomes a real card with its description and cover, and keeps your status, rating, notes, dates and tags. Rows that match nothing or several records stay custom cards, and the result screen lists the ambiguous ones.
- All imports share one progress dialog: you see the current row, the source being asked and running counts.
- New setting: show every tag on grid cards.
- The tag on a card uses the full width, and the "+N" count of other tags is always visible.
- The keyboard no longer pops up on its own in the tag dialogs on Android.
- Sort by release date.
- Watched date of an episode can be edited, unmarked episodes come back with Undo.
- Completed marks every episode of a series.
- Display fixes.
- Button and shortcut fixes.
