# 0.46.0

**めんどくせ 0.46**

**Cards show who made them.** Directors and studios on movies, creators and networks on series, developers and publishers on games. Tap a name to find the rest of its work in your collection. For cards you added earlier, press "Refresh from source".

- Custom cards get an Authors field, also in the CSV and JSON import.
- Title search finds a movie by its director and a game by its developer.
- Two new themes: PS1 Retro and Eva Azure, by shingo110.
- Emptied description, alternative title, genres and link of a custom card are saved.
- Episode stills come back after a broken download.
- Cache cleanup removes covers of deleted custom cards.
- Collection import tells you how many custom cards it skipped.
- After "Refresh from source", collections and search show the new data.
